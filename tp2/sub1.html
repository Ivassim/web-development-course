Ancient Wonders

Great Pyramid of Giza

Built around 2560 BC in Egypt by Pharaoh Khufu, it is the only ancient wonder still standing today.

Hanging Gardens of Babylon

Legendary terraced gardens built in Babylon (modern Iraq) by King Nebuchadnezzar II around 600 BC, though their existence is debated.

Statue of Zeus at Olympia

A giant gold and ivory statue of Zeus, created by sculptor Phidias around 435 BC in Greece.

Temple of Artemis at Ephesus

Located in Turkey, built around 550 BC, dedicated to the goddess Artemis. It was rebuilt several times before its destruction.

Mausoleum at Halicarnassus

Built in Turkey around 350 BC for Mausolus, a Persian satrap, it was famous for its grandeur and sculptures.

Colossus of Rhodes

A giant bronze statue of the sun god Helios, built around 280 BC in Greece, standing over 30 meters tall.

Lighthouse of Alexandria

Constructed on the island of Pharos, Egypt, around 280 BC to guide sailors safely into the harbor of Alexandria.

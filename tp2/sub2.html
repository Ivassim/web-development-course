New Wonders

Great Wall of China

Built over several dynasties, mainly during the Ming dynasty (14th–17th century), to protect China from invasions.

Petra

Ancient city carved into rose-colored rock in Jordan, developed by the Nabataeans around the 5th century BC.

Christ the Redeemer

Iconic statue in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, built between 1922 and 1931 to symbolize peace and Christianity.

Machu Picchu

Incan citadel in Peru, built in the 15th century by Emperor Pachacuti, hidden in the Andes mountains.

Chichén Itzá

Mayan city in Mexico, built around 600-1200 AD, famous for its pyramid and astronomical alignments.

Colosseum

Roman amphitheater in Rome, Italy, built between 70-80 AD, used for gladiatorial games and public spectacles.

Taj Mahal

White marble mausoleum in Agra, India, built by Emperor Shah Jahan between 1632-1653 in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
